Training of the SL700 booster (step 3)
  unit of prediction: one event (one row); labels are per event
  rows: SL700 train 344,271 (33,752 attack), negatives capped at 30:1
  replay OpTC: 12,500 attack + 25,000 benign;  replay ATLASv2: 2,233 attack + 35,267 benign
  init score (the booster learns the residual): 0.8*z1 for SL700 rows and replay negatives, 0.5*z1 for replay positives,
      z1 = 0.8*z_OpTC + z_ATLASv2 = frozen model of the previous step
  hard negatives (benign rows the previous model scores > 0.5): 565 rows, weight 5
  threshold: maximum F1 on SL700 validation; thresholds of ATLASv2 and OpTC are frozen from their own steps
